### About

Frontend engineer at **Equity Group Holdings**, where I work on **Equity Online** — the Angular digital banking platform serving Equity Bank customers. Day to day that means state management at scale, tight performance budgets, and interfaces that cannot afford to be wrong.

Alongside that I work on the ML side of fintech: fraud risk scoring, model deployment, and the plumbing between a trained model and a user-facing decision. I hold an **MSSE** from Quantic School of Business and Technology.

I came into engineering from quantity surveying — which is where I learned to care about precision before I ever wrote a line of code.

**Currently:** deepening my Angular architecture practice toward senior-level frontend work, while building and deploying ML services end to end — the goal is to own fintech products from the model to the screen.

### Featured Work

#### FraudPulse — real-time card fraud risk scoring

A production-shaped fraud decisioning service that scores card authorizations as **APPROVE**, **APPROVE_WITH_REVIEW**, or **DECLINE** on the synchronous authorization path, designed to a sub-200ms budget. Technical lead on a four-person team, owning integration, infrastructure, frontend delivery, and ML pipeline architecture.

- Signals-first **Angular** dashboard for analyst review
- **FastAPI + LightGBM** scoring service, Platt-scaled for calibrated probabilities
- Async post-transaction pipeline so model work never blocks the authorization
- **PostgreSQL** on Supabase, containerized CI/CD
